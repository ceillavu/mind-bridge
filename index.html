MindBridge Caregiver — Terms and Conditions & Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to MindBridge Caregiver ("MindBridge," "we," "us," or "our"). MindBridge Caregiver helps families caring for a loved one with dementia record daily observations in plain language, receive guidance, and track patterns over time. By using MindBridge Caregiver, you agree to these Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. What MindBridge Caregiver Is — and Is Not

  • Support Tool: MindBridge Caregiver is a support and organisational tool for caregivers to record observations and share summaries with healthcare professionals.
  • Not a Medical Device: MindBridge Caregiver is not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical assessment. AI-generated guidance or risk flags are informational only.
  • Pattern Observations, Not Diagnosis: Summaries note observed characteristics commonly associated with dementia presentations solely to support informed clinical discussions. Only a qualified clinician can diagnose dementia.
  • Not an Emergency Service: In immediate danger or medical emergencies, call emergency services (999) or visit the nearest emergency department immediately.

3. Data We Collect

We collect account data (name, email), care recipient data (nickname/first name, age, relationship), sensitive health diary entries, and usage logs.

Sensitive Personal Data: Under Malaysia's PDPA, health observations regarding memory, mood, and behavior are treated with heightened protection.

4. Legal Basis, Consent & Behalf Processing

Explicit consent is required under Section 40 of the PDPA. Where care recipients lack cognitive capacity, consent is obtained from the Caregiver as their legally authorised representative to protect their vital interests.

5. AI Processing & Cross-Border Transfer

Entries are processed by secure AI language model providers (which may operate outside Malaysia) solely to provide guidance, summaries, and pattern tracking. No personal data is sold or used for advertising.

6. Your Rights & Security

You maintain rights under PDPA to access, correct, delete, or request copies of recorded data. Data is encrypted in transit and securely partitioned.

MindBridge Check — Terms and Conditions & Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

MindBridge Check is a self-administered cognitive screening tool that lets an individual check their own cognitive baseline through short exercises (such as clock-drawing and word-recall tasks) and track results over time.

2. Screening Tool — Not Self-Diagnosis

  • Screening vs Diagnosis: MindBridge Check is a screening tool, not a diagnostic tool. It produces descriptive results (e.g. within/outside typical range) and never outputs a probability, stage, or named diagnostic condition.
  • Disclaimer: Every result screen notes: "This is a screening exercise, not a diagnosis. Speak with a doctor regarding memory or thinking concerns."
  • Target Users: Intended for adults aged 18+ for self-check or with consenting family members.

3. Data Collection & Family Screening Consent

Collects account info, screening score data, and timestamps. When testing a family member, explicit consent from that individual (or their legal guardian) is mandatory prior to test start.

4. Rights, Storage & Digital Health Alignment

Maintains PDPA compliance regarding data access, deletion, and cross-border encryption standard safeguards under Malaysian law.
